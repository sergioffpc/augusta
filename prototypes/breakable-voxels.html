PROTOTYPE · THROWAWAY

A Breakable Prop made of voxels

ADR-0071 keeps the Map fixed and breaks Breakable Props instead, each authored already broken into pieces. What if a Breakable Prop — a wall placed in the Map — held a grid of voxels instead? A round or an Explosion carves out the voxels it destroys, and the voxels no longer held up fall as pieces: large ones as Props the server spawns, small ones as Cosmetic debris. Does that give walls that open where they are hit, with few enough pieces and cheap enough rebuilds, and which rules decide what still stands? Click the wall to fire a round or set off an Explosion; the wall is seen from the front.

What this prototype assumes that no spec fixes
  • The Prop is 4 m wide, 3 m high and 0.25 m thick, on a 0.125 m grid seen from the front: 32 × 24 voxels, each the Prop's full thickness. A round or an Explosion destroys a voxel through its whole thickness.
  • A round puts half the energy it lost in the Prop into the voxel it strikes and an eighth into each of the four beside it; a round that strikes a hole passes through and loses nothing.
  • An Explosion's damage falls linearly from its centre to zero at its radius, and nothing shields a voxel from it.
  • An anchored edge is where the Prop meets the Map (floor, ceiling or a wall): a voxel on it is held by the Map.
  • Connectivity always applies: a voxel with no path of voxels to an anchored edge falls.
  • Overhang (experiment): a voxel held only by a path that goes sideways more than the limit falls; going up or down costs nothing.
  • Load (experiment): each held voxel passes its weight to the voxel that holds it, along one chosen path; a voxel carrying more than the limit is crushed and what it held falls or finds another path.
  • The fall is only the page's: each piece is a rigid body in the wall's plane that tumbles off the floor and what is left of the Prop, but passes through other pieces and cannot fall out of the plane. In the game a piece is a Prop the server's physics moves (ADR-0045).
  • Every rule is deterministic, so each client can repeat a hit and get the same wall; the numbers are stand-ins for Parameters (ADR-0039).
