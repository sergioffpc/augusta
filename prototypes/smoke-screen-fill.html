How a Smoke screen spreads through a Map

A smoke grenade bursts; every client grows the same cloud through the Map's free space on a 0.5 m grid. Does the fill decided in ADR-0072 (#840, #865, #1170) give the shape and the pace we want: round in the open, never through a wall or a wall corner, stopping short in a long corridor, thick at once, clearing from its edges in? Click the map to throw a smoke grenade at the current time, then move time and watch.

What this prototype assumes that the spec does not fix
  • The Map is a 72.5 m × 43 m test Map: a yard 8 m high beside a building of six rooms, 3 m high, either side of a corridor; a grenade bursts 0.25 m above the floor. The grid is 3D; the map is seen from above.
  • Clearing: voxels start thinning one after another, evenly spread over rank, the last reached first; each thins over the ramp time, so the first reached is gone exactly at growing + holding + clearing.
  • Rise is an experiment, not in the spec: a step up costs that percentage less and a step down that percentage more, so the cloud rises and fills ceilings first.
  • The volume curve's N is the cloud's target voxel count (volume ÷ 0.125 m³) by default; a cloud stopped short by its reach then fills on the curve's early, fast part. Switch it under Parameters to the voxels actually in the plan.
  • The source is the free voxel nearest the burst point within 4 voxels (2 m) on each axis, ties to the lower index.
  • The seed comes from the burst point and the time it burst; the Smoke screen voxel bound is a stand-in of 16 384 voxels (2 048 m³).
